title: "GraphPCA: a fast and interpretable dimension reduction algorithm for spatial transcriptomics data"
collection: publications
category: manuscripts
permalink: /publication/2024-graphpca
excerpt: >-
  Standard PCA uses gene expression but ignores where cells or spots sit in a tissue. We developed GraphPCA to learn low-dimensional embeddings with a spatial neighborhood graph that encourages nearby locations to have similar scores. The model retains interpretable gene loadings and has a closed-form solution. We evaluated it on simulated and real data for spatial domain detection and illustrated its use for denoising and trajectory inference.
