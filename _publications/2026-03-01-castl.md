title: "Castl: robust identification of spatially variable genes in spatial transcriptomics via an ensemble-based framework"
collection: publications
category: manuscripts
permalink: /publication/2026-castl
excerpt: >-
  Different spatial tests can flag different genes in the same tissue. We developed Castl to combine evidence from seven kernel- and graph-based detectors using three strategies: pooling ranks, combining p-values, and estimating false discoveries with permuted genes as negative controls. We evaluated the ensemble on simulated and real tissues, including how the selected genes help distinguish spatial domains.
