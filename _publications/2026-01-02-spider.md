category: manuscripts
permalink: /publication/2026-spider
excerpt: >-
  To benchmark spatial transcriptomics methods, we need tissue-like data whose underlying organization is known. We built Spider to simulate cell layouts from target cell-type proportions and probabilities of neighboring cell types. Batched simulated annealing assigns cell types to locations; the simulator then attaches expression profiles and can aggregate cells into spots. We used these controlled datasets to compare spatial clustering and spot deconvolution methods.
