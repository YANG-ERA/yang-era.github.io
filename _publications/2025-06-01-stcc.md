title: "STCC enhances spatial domain detection through consensus clustering of spatial transcriptomics data"
collection: publications
category: manuscripts
permalink: /publication/2025-stcc
excerpt: >-
  Spatial clustering tools can assign the same sample to different tissue domains. We developed STCC to turn multiple partitions into one consensus map, using strategies ranging from co-clustering averages to hypergraphs and weighted nonnegative matrix factorization. Across seven benchmark datasets, consensus often improved on individual runs and made domain assignments less sensitive to the chosen cluster count.
